        A Cath Lab for accurate answers, close to home

        सही जाँच, सुरक्षित इलाज।

        Chest pain, breathlessness or an abnormal ECG need clear answers fast. Our Cath Lab lets an interventional cardiologist see your arteries and treat a blockage in the same hospital, without a long journey.

        Call 73798 80000
        1. Check your heartECG, Echo and a TMT show how your heart is working and whether you need further tests.
        2. See the arteriesAngiography maps the blood vessels so the doctor can find any blockage.
        3. Open the blockage, if neededAngioplasty treats a blocked artery. Your cardiologist explains the options before any procedure.

        Questions patients ask us

        Something else? Call 73798 80000.

        Which services does the hospital provide?

        Cardiology, Nephrology (kidney diseases), ENT, Orthopaedics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, General Surgery, Urology, ICU, emergency and diagnostic services.

        Can I get all my heart problems treated here?

        Yes. We handle diagnosis of heart disease, angiography, angioplasty and ECG, along with treatment for blood pressure, cholesterol and irregular heartbeat.

        Are emergency and ICU services available 24×7?

        Yes. Emergency and ICU services run round the clock with advanced life-saving equipment and trained personnel.

        Does the hospital accept Ayushman Bharat cards?

        Yes. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) holders are entitled to cashless treatment as per government guidelines. Ask at the help desk about eligibility and documents.
