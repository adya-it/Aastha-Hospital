        A Cath Lab for accurate answers, close to home

        सही जाँच, सुरक्षित इलाज।

        Chest pain, breathlessness or an abnormal ECG need clear answers fast. Our Cath Lab lets an interventional cardiologist see your arteries and treat a blockage in the same hospital, without a long journey.

        1. Check your heartECG, Echo and a TMT show how your heart is working and whether you need further tests.
        2. See the arteriesAngiography maps the blood vessels so the doctor can find any blockage.
        3. Open the blockage, if neededAngioplasty treats a blocked artery. Your cardiologist explains the options before any procedure.

        Health tips from our doctors

        Know the warning signs of a heart attack

        Chest pressure or pain, discomfort spreading to the arm, jaw or back, breathlessness, cold sweat or nausea. Do not wait it out. Call 73798 80000 or get to the emergency desk.

        Check your blood pressure regularly

        High blood pressure often has no symptoms. A quick check at your next visit helps catch it early, before it strains your heart and kidneys.

        Do not ignore swelling or changes in urine

        Lasting puffiness of the face or feet, or a change in how often you pass urine, is worth a check with a kidney specialist.
